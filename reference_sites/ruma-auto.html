RUMA Medical

Welcome to RUMA Medical, where we focus on helping you achieve natural, lasting results through injectables, skincare, functional medicine, and intimate health services. Real beauty starts on the inside, and that’s why we look at the full picture of your health and wellness, not just what you see in the mirror.

Shelby Miller, DNP, FNP-C, founded RUMA in 2018 after years of working in plastic surgery and mastering the art of injectables. She built this practice on the belief that aesthetic care should be integrated with real medical expertise.
